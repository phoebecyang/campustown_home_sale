BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Clear rules.
Honest comparisons.

Every figure comes from the provided College-town home sales dataset. Here is how we use it.

Read the data description ↗
Which sales are included?

Only records labeled Champaign or Urbana in Illinois. Sale prices must be between $10,000 and $5,000,000 inclusive, to limit implausible price records. The bedroom filter applies to every comparison. No records are removed for sharing an address; listing_id identifies each sale. Date coverage and exclusion counts are computed from the data above.

How are the metrics calculated?

Medians are the middle value, or the average of the two middle values. Price per square foot is the median of each sale's price divided by its living area, not the ratio of city medians. Living-area metrics use only positive, reported square footage. Days on market is contract date minus list date; both must be present and the interval nonnegative. Same-day contracts count as zero. Sample sizes appear beside each metric.

What should I keep in mind?

Missing listing dates are not random, so selling times describe only sales with usable dates. The source has not been verified against county records. Property type is unreliable and is not used. Lot size, bathrooms, taxes, and assessed value are not used because of documented anomalies. No claim about affordability, current prices, or the cause of a city difference is made.
